# Human Skill Extinction Predictor

## Project Overview

### Problem Statement
An AI-powered platform that predicts which professional skills will grow, remain stable, or become obsolete over the next 5–10 years using labor market signals, technology adoption trends, and hiring demand data.

The system helps students, professionals, recruiters, and organizations identify future-proof skills and make informed career decisions.

---

# Objectives

## Primary Objectives

1. Identify future-proof skills
2. Detect declining and obsolete skills
3. Forecast long-term hiring demand
4. Recommend valuable skills to learn
5. Provide career transition guidance
6. Analyze country-wise skill demand patterns
7. Generate personalized learning roadmaps

---

# Expected Outcomes

The platform should be able to answer:

- Which skills are growing fastest?
- Which skills are at risk of becoming obsolete?
- What should a Data Analyst learn next?
- Which technologies will dominate the next decade?
- How does skill demand differ across countries?
- What career transitions are recommended?

# Proposed Skill Extinction Score (SES)

## Goal

Create a unique metric that becomes the core innovation of the project.

## Inputs

- Hiring Demand Trend
- GitHub Growth
- Google Trend Growth
- Salary Trend
- Industry Adoption

## Output

0–10 Score

### Interpretation

0–2
High Extinction Risk

3–5
Stable but Declining

6–8
Growing

9–10
Future-Proof
