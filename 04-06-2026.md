### LinkedIn Data Investigation & Validation

Performed detailed validation of LinkedIn datasets used for SES generation.

Key findings:

* Occupational taxonomy datasets contained role categories rather than technical skills.
* Industry mappings represented business functions instead of technologies.
* Direct skill joins produced incorrect feature mappings.
* Generic workforce datasets introduced significant noise into skill-level analysis.

Examples discovered:

```text
Advertising
Finance
Distribution
Product Management
```

These represented job domains rather than technical competencies required for SES computation.

---

### Demand Extraction Improvements

Initial demand calculations produced inflated values due to simple substring matching.

Examples:

```text
go
rag
rust
```

appeared significantly overrepresented because they matched unrelated words inside job descriptions.

Implemented:

* Regex-based skill matching
* Exact token boundary validation
* False-positive reduction strategy

This significantly improved skill demand estimation quality.
