# Career Intelligence Engine Research

## Objective

The objective of this phase was to study modern AI-powered recommendation systems and investigate how Large Language Models (LLMs) and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) can be integrated into the Human Skill Extinction Predictor (HSEP) to provide personalized career guidance.

Rather than implementing a complete Career Advisor, this phase focused on understanding the architecture, components, and techniques required for building an explainable AI-driven career recommendation system.

---

## Research Areas Explored

### Large Language Models (LLMs)

Studied how modern LLMs can be used for:

* Career guidance
* Skill recommendations
* Industry insights
* Workforce intelligence explanations
* Personalized learning roadmaps

Key concepts explored:

* Prompt Engineering
* Context Injection
* Grounded Generation
* Hallucination Reduction
* Explainable Recommendations

---

### Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)

Studied the architecture of RAG systems and how they combine structured knowledge with language models.

Typical workflow:

```text
User Query
      ↓
Retrieval System
      ↓
Relevant Knowledge
      ↓
Context Construction
      ↓
LLM
      ↓
Generated Response
```

Benefits:

* Reduces hallucinations
* Uses project-specific knowledge
* Produces more accurate recommendations
* Improves explainability

## Key Learnings

* LLMs alone are insufficient for reliable workforce intelligence recommendations.
* Retrieval mechanisms are necessary to ground responses in project data.
* RAG architectures provide a practical approach for combining structured analytics with natural language generation.
* Explainability is critical for career recommendation systems.
* Workforce intelligence outputs generated by HSEP can serve as an effective retrieval knowledge base.
