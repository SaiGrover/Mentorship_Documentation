## Skill Extinction Score (SES)

The Skill Extinction Score (SES) is a proprietary metric designed to estimate the future relevance of a professional skill by combining multiple workforce and technology adoption indicators.

The objective of SES is to classify skills into categories such as:

* High Extinction Risk
* Stable
* Growing
* Future-Proof

### Proposed Formula

```text
SES =
0.35 × Demand Growth
+ 0.25 × GitHub Growth
+ 0.15 × Google Trend Growth
+ 0.15 × Industry Adoption
+ 0.10 × Salary Growth
```

### Weight Selection Rationale

The weights were assigned based on the relative importance of each factor in determining long-term skill relevance.

| Factor              | Weight | Justification                                                           |
| ------------------- | ------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Demand Growth       | 35%    | Strongest indicator of real-world market demand and hiring trends       |
| GitHub Growth       | 25%    | Reflects technology adoption and developer ecosystem growth             |
| Google Trend Growth | 15%    | Captures emerging interest and technology awareness                     |
| Industry Adoption   | 15%    | Measures how widely a skill is used across industries and organizations |
| Salary Growth       | 10%    | Indicates economic value but acts as a lagging market signal            |

### Why Demand Growth Receives the Highest Weight

Hiring demand directly reflects the needs of employers and industry. If organizations continue to recruit for a skill, it is likely to remain relevant in the future.

Examples:

* Generative AI
* MLOps
* Data Engineering

These skills exhibit strong hiring demand and therefore receive higher SES values.

### Why GitHub Growth Receives Significant Weight

GitHub activity serves as an indicator of technology adoption and ecosystem development.

Metrics include:

* Repository Growth
* Stars
* Contributors
* Open Source Activity

Rapid ecosystem growth often precedes widespread industry adoption.

### Why Google Trends and Industry Adoption Receive Moderate Weight

Google Trends captures public and professional interest in a technology, while Industry Adoption measures the breadth of usage across organizations and sectors.

Together, they provide supporting evidence of a skill's future potential.

### Why Salary Growth Receives the Lowest Weight

Salary is considered a lagging indicator because compensation can be influenced by temporary market conditions, talent shortages, or niche specialization.

Therefore, salary contributes to the overall score but does not dominate the prediction.

### Score Interpretation

| SES Range | Classification       |
| --------- | -------------------- |
| 0 – 2     | High Extinction Risk |
| 3 – 5     | Stable but Declining |
| 6 – 8     | Growing              |
| 9 – 10    | Future-Proof         |

### Future Improvements

The current weights are based on domain assumptions and workforce analytics principles.

Future versions of SES may determine weights automatically using:

* Random Forest Feature Importance
* XGBoost Feature Importance
* SHAP Analysis
* Regression-Based Weight Learning

This would make the scoring framework more data-driven and scientifically robust.
