# Skill Classification Framework, Archetype Discovery & SES Redesign

**Date:** 09 June 2026

---

# Objective

Transform the expanded skill universe into a structured technology intelligence framework by introducing skill classification, refining the Skill Extinction Score (SES) methodology, and defining the roadmap for clustering and forecasting-based workforce intelligence.

---

# Background

The previous day's analysis revealed several critical observations:

* The original skill universe contained only 35 skills.
* Workforce intelligence signals lacked temporal depth.
* Large-scale skill extraction generated more than 39,000 unique skill entities.
* The extracted universe contained technical skills, soft skills, certifications, workforce attributes, and substantial noise.

These findings required a redesign of the HSEP pipeline before proceeding to forecasting and SES computation.

---

# Review of Existing SES Methodology

The original SES framework followed:

```text
LinkedIn Demand
        +
Salary Premium
        +
Current Usage
        +
Future Interest
        +
Global Adoption Score
        +
Growth Intelligence
        ↓
Feature Matrix
        ↓
XGBoost
        ↓
SHAP
        ↓
Skill Extinction Score
```

During review, a major limitation was identified.

The proposed SES target was still heavily dependent on manually engineered assumptions.

As a result, machine learning models would largely learn predefined human logic rather than discovering meaningful workforce patterns.

---

# SES Redesign Discussion

A new direction was proposed.

Instead of directly predicting SES using a synthetic target:

```text

        ↓

        ↓
XGBoost
```

the project would move toward discovering natural workforce archetypes through unsupervised learning.

# Unsupervised Learning Framework

A clustering-based approach was selected.

Workflow:

```text
Feature Matrix
        ↓
Scaling
        ↓
K-Means Clustering
        ↓
Skill Archetype Discovery
        ↓
SES Computation
```

This approach allows workforce patterns to emerge directly from the data rather than from manually assigned labels.

---

# Clustering Evaluation

Multiple cluster configurations were evaluated.

Methods:

* Elbow Method
* Silhouette Score Analysis

Metrics were computed across several cluster counts.

Observations:

* Inertia decreased continuously as cluster count increased.
* Silhouette analysis indicated meaningful separation among workforce intelligence groups.
* Four clusters provided an interpretable balance between simplicity and skill segmentation.

Selected:

```text
K = 4
```
