The main goal was:

```text
Make every important graph understandable by explaining what it shows and what insight the user should take from it.
```

# Graph Interpretation Addition

Interpretation sections were added for graphs across the website.

The purpose of these interpretations is to help users understand the meaning behind the visualizations instead of leaving them to interpret technical charts on their own.

Each interpretation focuses on explaining:

* What the graph represents
* What pattern or trend is visible
* Why the graph is important
* What conclusion the user can draw from it

This improves the explainability of the platform.

# Explainability Improvement

Adding graph interpretations improved the overall explainability of SkillLens AI.

Previously, some users could see the visual pattern but might not understand its importance.

The updated approach follows:

```text
Graph
↓
Visible Pattern
↓
Interpretation
↓
Practical Insight
```

For example, instead of only showing that a skill trend is increasing, the interpretation explains what this may indicate about future demand or career relevance.

---

# Interpretation Writing Approach

The graph interpretations were kept:

* Short
* Clear
* Data-focused
* Easy to understand
* Connected to the purpose of the graph
* Free from unnecessary technical language

The goal was not to describe every point in the graph.

Instead, each interpretation highlights the most useful insight.

Example structure:

```text
Interpretation:

The graph shows increasing demand for several AI and data-related skills. Skills with consistently rising trends may provide stronger future career opportunities, while declining trends may require closer evaluation before long-term specialization.
```

This format makes analytical results more accessible to users.
